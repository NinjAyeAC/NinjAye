# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must keep technical discussion respectful, evidence-based and
focused on the repository. Harassment, threats, doxxing, credential sharing,
malware distribution and publication of another person's private data are not
acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code and evidence rather than individuals.
- Use synthetic or redacted samples.
- Respect software licenses and service terms.
- Report security issues privately.
- Accept correction when a claim is not supported by reproducible evidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when
behavior violates this standard or creates a security or privacy risk. Reports
should be sent privately to the repository maintainers through the contact
method published on the hosting account or organization.
